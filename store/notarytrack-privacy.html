NotaryTrack — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

The short version

NotaryTrack does not collect any data. Everything stays on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

How the app works

  • Your clients, signings, fees and notes are stored only on your device
  • The app works fully offline; it never requests your location or any other data
  • Contacts access happens only when you explicitly choose to import a client from your address book — nothing is read automatically

What we never do

  • We never upload, transmit, or sell anything
  • We never track you, profile you, or share data with third parties
  • There is nothing to leak: the app has no network access at all

Data removal

Deleting the app removes all of your records immediately and completely. There is no server copy, because nothing was ever sent anywhere.
